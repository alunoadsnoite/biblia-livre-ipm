Bíblia Livre IPM is an independent edition available on F-Droid.

The app interface and bundled content are available only in Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR). The app does not provide a language selector; this English listing does not indicate an English interface.

Four Bible translations, Salmos e Hinos (1880), notes, highlights, favorites, search, reading plans, Android widget and device text-to-speech. No bundled document library or song compilation. User songs and bulletins stay on the device.

Offline voices must be installed in the operating system. Optional local reminders and JSON backups.

A distinct Android ID allows installation alongside Bíblia IPM.
